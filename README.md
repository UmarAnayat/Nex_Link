### The Idea

> A call should start before you overthink it.  
> Nexlink makes that the default.

**Nexlink** is a focused calling product for one-to-one voice and video.  
It brings clean entry, dual-mode calling, and an in-call experience that stays out of the way — designed for speed, clarity, and zero friction.
